Deliverable 05

Design Logic & Rationale

{ }

Code Bracket Icon

The custom mark uses angular code brackets — universal symbols of programming — with a circular accent node representing the "Alpha" pivot point. It reads instantly as a tech company.

Aa

Syne Typeface

Syne's geometric structure and ultra-bold weight gives the wordmark authority and memorability. Its slight quirk at high weights avoids feeling corporate or generic.

■

Electric Blue

Chosen for its association with intelligence, trust, and digital precision. It pops on both dark and light backgrounds without relying on gradients or effects.

●

Red Accent

The small red node within the mark adds energy and tension. It differentiates CodeAlpha from competitors who rely on monochromatic palettes.
